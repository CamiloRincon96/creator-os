# Brand rules (template)

`/creator-brand` fills it in from your product's repo or website. The rules here outrank the shared ones in `WORKFLOW.md`.

- **Name and how it's written:** <…>
- **Tone:** <how the brand talks: warm, technical, funny…>. Language and variant: <…>.
- **Design:** colors (hex), typefaces and logo. They're in `reel/src/brand.ts` and in `identity/`.
- **Identities:** which style from `production/styles/` each type of video uses. A brand can have several, for example one for to-camera reels with animation and another for covers: <type of video → style>.
- **What can be shown:** <what the product does today>.
- **What isn't said or shown:** <promises, competitors, sensitive data…>.
- **Facts:** figures, features and taglines come from the product's repo or website. They aren't made up.
