## The content wheel

| Role | What for | What it looks like | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shareable** | Reach new people | "This happens to me", everyday humor, a fact you want to send to someone | Most of the reels |
| **Useful** | Get saves and return visits | Mini guides that help even if they don't use your product | ~2 or 3 out of 10 |
| **Connection** | Get follows | Behind the scenes, what went wrong, polls | Stories daily |
| **Product** | Sales or sign-ups | The product solving something in 3 seconds | ~1 out of 10 |
