## What data it uses

## Where it's stored

On my computer. The access keys stay in the operating system's secure store: the macOS Keychain, the Windows Credential Manager or secret-tool on Linux. They never go in shared files.

## Deleting the data

To remove access and delete the data:
1. Remove the app's permission in each network's settings:
   - Facebook and Instagram: Settings → Business integrations;
   - Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions;
   - TikTok: Settings → Security → Apps and services.
2. Delete the keys from the system's secure store.
