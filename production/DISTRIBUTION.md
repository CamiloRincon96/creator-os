## The accounts and their role

| Account | Role | What it posts | Rhythm (target) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Your personal account** | The center of trust: people follow people before logos | What you build and learn, your day to day, your opinion | 3 or 4 reels a week, stories daily |
| **Your main brand** | The one that most needs to grow | The wheel from its `NICHE.md`: lots of shareable, some useful, connection and a little product | 1 reel a day (1 big one and the rest express), stories daily, 1 or 2 carousels a week |
| **Other brands** | Keep them from going dark | Their niche's content | 1 or 2 a week |

## How a piece travels between accounts

1. If you're on camera, it's posted as a **collaboration** (Collab) between your personal account and the brand: it shows up on both profiles.
2. Animated or express pieces go on the brand only. Your personal account shares them in stories with a mention.
3. Never the same identical video on two accounts separately, except as a collaboration.
4. TikTok and YouTube Shorts: the same reels, uploaded natively and without a watermark.
