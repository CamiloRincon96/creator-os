# Privacy policy: Creator OS plugin

Last updated: November 16, 2026.

## What the plugin collects

Nothing. The Creator OS plugin has no telemetry, no analytics, no hooks, no MCP servers and no background processes. It doesn't read, store or send personal data, and Dirac Core receives no data from it.

## What it does on your computer

- It looks in your working folder (and in `~/creator-os`) for a Creator OS workspace.
- With your yes, it runs `git clone https://github.com/CamiloRincon96/creator-os` into a folder you choose. That is a download from GitHub; GitHub's own privacy policy applies to that request.
- If git is missing, it shows you the install command for your system and runs it only with your yes.

## After the workspace is set up

The workspace is open source and runs on your computer. Your brands, ideas, videos and metrics stay in that folder. Some optional features talk to third-party services only when you turn them on and approve them, and each one is under that service's own policy:

- the Instagram Graph API, to read metrics and publish the batches you approve;
- ScrapeCreators, through the last30days plugin, for niche research;
- ElevenLabs, Higgsfield and Adobe, paid tools that are never used without a quote you approved.

Keys and tokens are saved by you in your keychain or environment variables; Creator OS never writes them into its files.
